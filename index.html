XBOX ONE & KEYBOARD CONTROLS

P1 GROUND SILOS (PAD 1 / MOUSE)

  • Aim: Left Stick / Mouse
  • Nearest Silo: RT / Left-Click
  • Left / Center / Right: X(LB) / A / B(RB) or Keys 1 / 2 / 3

P2 ENGINEER TRUCK (GROUND CREW)

  • Drive: P2 Left Stick / A-D or Arrows
  • Hold Weld: RT or A / Space or J
  • Hold Shield: X or RB / K or E
  • Turbo Boost: B or LT / Shift
  • Costs: Reload(1) • Shield(2) • Patch(2) • Silo(3) • Rebuild(4)

P2 AIR SUPPORT (AIR CO-OP)

  • Swap Ship: Y / Key E
  • Asteroids: Stick Rotate, LT Thrust, RT/A Fire, X Hyperspace
  • Defender: Stick Fly, LT Boost, RT/A Laser, B Smart Bomb
  • Keyboard: WASD Move, Space Fire, Shift Boost, Q Special

1P HYBRID MODE (SOLO)

  • Xbox Pad 1: Left Stick flies ship, Right Stick aims silos, LT Thrust, LB Ship Fire, RT Silo Fire, Y Swap Ship, X/B Special.
  • PC: Mouse aims & fires silos while WASD + Space + Shift + Q + E flies Air Support.
